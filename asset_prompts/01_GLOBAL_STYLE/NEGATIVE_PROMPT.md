# Global exclusion prompt

Exclude: photorealism, detailed anime painting, pixel art, gritty texture, horror damage, cinematic camera tilt, fisheye perspective, excessive bloom, glossy plastic rendering, dense ornamental patterns, cropped subject, extra limbs, inconsistent faces, random accessories, illegible small detail, text, letters, numbers, logos, signatures, watermarks, baked checkerboard, unwanted opaque background on a cutout, extra objects not requested.

Do not remove intentional subject markings that are required by the approved reference. Do not erase necessary contact shapes in the name of simplification.
