# Global visual style prompt

Cute chibi cartoon 2D game art, clean rounded silhouettes, warm pastel palette, clear contour, flat color with at most two gentle shading steps. Minimal texture, readable at small size. Cheerful but calm, without excessive sparkle.

Orthographic top-down oblique view, approximately 45-degree camera elevation, front-facing façades visible, square world grid axes. No isometric diamond-grid presentation. Consistent soft light from upper left. Keep cast shadows separate when requested.

Palette cues: cream #FFF9 EE, sage #A9 C5 A0, forest #416 B54, blush #F2 B7 BF, warm yellow #F5 D58 A, pale sky #BFDCE5, warm brown #96755 B. Character proportions about 2.5 heads tall, head approximately 40% of height.

Ruined assets remain cute and recoverable: overgrown plants, faded paint, missing small boards, gentle age. Restored assets feel cared for without changing the architecture. Preserve negative space and interaction readability.

Use approved references as the final authority for identity and proportions. No photorealism, anime rendering, pixel art, painterly noise, dramatic cinematic perspective or heavy gradient lighting.
